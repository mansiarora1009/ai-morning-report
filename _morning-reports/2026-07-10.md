# Morning Report — 2026-07-10

## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

---

### 1. ChatGPT Work + GPT-5.6 "Ultra Mode" — OpenAI's biggest product shift yet

**What it is:** OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 (three sizes: Sol, Terra, Luna) alongside ChatGPT Work — a full workplace agent that connects to Slack, Gmail, Drive, CRMs, and internal files to execute multi-step tasks with minimal human input. The new "ultra mode" goes beyond single-agent: it coordinates 4 agents in parallel by default, trading token spend for faster results on hard tasks.

**Why it matters:** This isn't just a new model — it's a behavioral shift in how people will use AI at work. The default is now *delegation and parallel execution*, not back-and-forth chat. GPT-5.6 Terra already beats Fable 5 at ¼ the cost; Luna beats Opus 4.8 in speed and efficiency.

**Format:** Shift

**Reel angle:** "AI just stopped being a chatbot — here's what workplace AI actually looks like in 2026."

---

### 2. Karpathy's workflow flip: from 80/20 coding himself to 20/80 delegating to agents

**What it is:** Karpathy publicly documented his shift at Sequoia Ascent 2026: he now delegates ~80% of work to agents, down from doing 80% himself in late 2024. His framing: "idle tokens = you're the bottleneck." He calls this the "Software 3.0" era — where the context window is your program and English is your language.

**Why it matters:** When Karpathy says his workflow changed this dramatically, it's a signal worth taking seriously. Most tech workers are still working *serially* with AI (one prompt, one reply). Running agents in parallel, like he describes, is a full professional-generation gap.

**Format:** Shift

**Reel angle:** "Karpathy flipped from doing 80% himself to delegating 80% to agents — here's what that actually means for your workflow."

---

### 3. Grok 4.5 — the first frontier model co-trained with an IDE

**What it is:** SpaceXAI (xAI post-Cursor acquisition) launched Grok 4.5, described as "the first model trained specifically for coding and agents." It was co-developed with Cursor — meaning the IDE's usage patterns shaped the model training, not just integration. Positioned as Opus-class but faster and cheaper; available in Cursor now with double usage for the first week.

**Why it matters:** This is a new development: not just "model X is now available in tool Y," but a model purpose-built from training for agentic IDE workflows. If this approach works, every serious coding tool may need its own model variant to stay competitive.

**Format:** Explainer

**Reel angle:** "Why the AI coding wars are now about training, not just integration."
