## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. Anthropic Engineering: "Scaling Managed Agents — Decoupling the brain from the hands"
**What it is:** Anthropic published an engineering post on how their managed agents are architected: the reasoning model ("brain") is separated from the tool-execution layer ("hands"). The brain plans and decides; the hands call tools, run code, interact with systems. These layers communicate through a structured interface and can be updated independently.

**Why it matters:** This is the actual design pattern that production AI systems are converging on — and most people building with AI haven't named it or understood it yet. The moment you see the separation clearly, you start building agents that are easier to debug, more reliable, and cheaper to iterate on. Your audience is likely handing their model a list of 20 tools and wondering why it breaks.
