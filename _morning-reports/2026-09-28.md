### 2. Jev and "Decision Models" — A New Shape of AI Explainer
**What it is:** TypeSafe AI's Jev just raised $1B at a $10B valuation for a model that doesn't output text at all. Instead: text in → typed probabilities out. You ask yes/no questions ("Is this spam?"), choice questions ("Which category?"), or score questions ("Rate this 1–10"). Output is a float. No hallucinations structurally possible. Costs $0.042/M tokens (cheaper than GPT-5 Nano). Simon Willison calls them "decision models."

**Why it matters:** This is a genuinely different tool mental model: not "prompt the AI to answer" but "query the AI to classify." Use cases: spam detection, search reranking, prioritization, routing. Open-weight alternatives already exist (CLM, tev1, GLiNER2.5). The $10B valuation means this space is real.

**Format:** Explainer — makes a vague new term actually make sense.

**Reel angle:** "Jev is the AI that never generates text — here's why that's actually useful."
