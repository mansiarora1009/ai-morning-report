## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. Your Agent Now Has Your Keys
**What it is:** Nous Research's Hermes Agent can now browse the web using a managed copy of your *real* Chrome profile — your logins, cookies, saved sessions. No manual auth, no OAuth flows. The agent acts as you.

**Why it matters:** This is the auth friction collapse that browser agents have needed for two years. But it's also a fundamentally different risk surface: an agent with your real session can do anything you can. Scoped permissions were always the right design; now they're urgent.

**Format:** Shift

**Reel angle:** "AI agents just got your house keys — here's what that actually means"

---

### 2. The Scaffold Beats the Model
**What it is:** New research (JIT-Agent) shows models that synthesize a custom harness — memory, planning, tool orchestration — on the fly outperform off-the-shelf agents running a fixed scaffold. Separately, work on finite-state machine induction from agent traces suggests behavior topology is shaped more by the deployment scaffold than the underlying LLM. Anthropic's "Scaling Managed Agents" post frames the same idea as "decoupling the brain from the hands."

**Why it matters:** This reframes the "which model is best?" question. The container you put the model in matters as much as the model itself — which is why Claude Code, Cursor, and Perplexity feel so different even running the same base model.

**Format:** Explainer

**Reel angle:** "Stop optimizing your AI model. Start optimizing your scaffold."

### 3. Cursor Is Now Part of SpaceX
**What it is:** Cursor announced it has joined SpaceX. The AI coding editor — one of the first tools to hit mass adoption for AI-assisted dev — is now inside one of the most technically demanding engineering orgs on earth.

**Why it matters:** This isn't just an acquisition story. It signals that enterprises at the frontier aren't waiting for AI tools to mature — they're absorbing the companies building them. The AI dev tooling land grab has moved from investment rounds to ownership.

**Reel angle:** "Cursor just joined SpaceX. The AI coding tool era just entered a new phase."
