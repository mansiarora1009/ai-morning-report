## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. OpenAI DevDay 2026 — "Sign in with ChatGPT" is the real story
**What it is**: OpenAI's DevDay keynote introduced several products, but the one with lasting structural impact is *Sign in with ChatGPT* — users authenticate into third-party apps and spend their own ChatGPT tokens there. Like Sign in with Apple, but for AI compute.

**Why it matters**: This reframes the business model for AI-native app builders. Instead of paying for API calls yourself, your users bring their own credits. It's also a moat play: the more apps that adopt it, the harder it becomes to switch away from ChatGPT.

**Format**: **Shift** — the biggest behavioral/business shift of DevDay, not the flashiest demo.

**Reel angle**: "OpenAI DevDay announced a lot. But this one thing quietly changes how AI apps get built."

---

### 2. Cursor SDK adds mid-run steering and background subagents
**What it is**: The Cursor SDK update lets you redirect agents mid-task, spawn background subagents that report back to a parent, replace system prompts on the fly, and annotate custom MCP tools as `readOnlyHint` or `destructiveHint` so the agent knows what's risky.

**Why it matters**: This is the first time coding agents start to feel like something you *manage*, not just *launch and hope*. Mid-run steering in particular signals a shift: instead of agents being fire-and-forget, they become interruptible, resumable, and controllable. That changes workflows.

**Format**: **Explainer** — most people using Cursor haven't seen what the SDK now enables.

**Reel angle**: "Cursor's new SDK changes what 'AI agent' actually means — you can steer it mid-task now."

---

### 3. Hard budget caps are becoming the default — here's why it matters for agent builders
**What it is**: Simon Willison made the case that AI agents spinning up paid services (AWS, APIs, hosted apps) need hard budget caps as the *default*, not a warning email. AWS launched spending limits in September; Google Cloud launched Spend Caps in July. The argument: soft caps and warning emails don't protect you from a rogue agent running overnight.

**Why it matters**: Anyone building or using coding agents in production is one runaway subprocess away from a surprise four-figure bill. This is an underappreciated infrastructure-literacy issue for the audience.

**Format**: **Tip** — immediately actionable: check your AWS/GCP/API budget settings right now.

**Reel angle**: "Your AI agent can bankrupt you while you sleep. Here's the one setting you need to turn on."
