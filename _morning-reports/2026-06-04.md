## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. Anthropic's June 15 Agent Metering Change — Claude subscriptions are splitting in two

**What it is:** Starting June 15, programmatic Claude usage (Agent SDK, `claude -p` non-interactive, Claude Code GitHub Actions, third-party apps like OpenClaw) moves to a separate monthly credit pool — no longer drawing from the same bucket as chat. Credits scale with subscription tier: Pro = $20/mo, Max 5x = $100, Max 20x = $200. Credits don't roll over, and you can't dip into chat limits when they run out.

**Why it matters to her audience:** This is the moment Claude's pricing acknowledges that agents and humans aren't the same customer. Anyone running agents through subscriptions cheaply (a $20 Pro sub could run $100s of API-equivalent work via OpenClaw) loses that arbitrage on June 15. The era of "subscription-priced agents" is ending, and usage discipline starts now.

**Reel angle:** *"Anthropic just split Claude into two products — and if you've been running agents on a $20 sub, you need to see this before June 15."*

### 2. Satya Nadella: "We Need Tokens, Not Headcount" — the meta-work reframe

**What it is:** In a live podcast at Microsoft Build (Latent Space × No Priors crossover), Nadella described his Azure networking team's response to a new AI system managing fiber operations: instead of requesting more staff, they said "we need tokens to manage our operation." He called this shift to "meta work" — using AI to do the metacognition your team used to do — the model for every organization.

His other key claim: private evaluation sets may be the most valuable IP a company can build. "Every company having private evals may be the biggest driver of IP... it may be one of the biggest drivers" — because unlike human tacit knowledge, AI-learned expertise stored as traces and agent memory can go on a balance sheet.

**Why it matters to her audience:** Most content about AI at work is vague ("AI will change jobs"). This is a CEO-level reframe with a concrete mechanism: the unit of organizational intelligence is shifting from headcount to token throughput. And "build your evals" is one of the most underrated, actionable pieces of advice in the current moment.

**Format:** Shift + Explainer

**Reel angle:** *"Microsoft's CEO said his team stopped asking for headcount — they asked for tokens instead. Here's what that shift actually means for your work."*

### 3. Uber's $1,500/mo AI Spend Cap + Harvey's Hybrid Routing Numbers — cost management is now a core AI skill

**What it is:** Uber is capping AI coding-agent spend at $1,500/month per employee per tool (via @simonw). At the same time, Harvey (legal AI) published real hybrid routing data: a GLM 5.1 (cheaper) + Claude Opus 4.7 (frontier advisor) combo hit **18% all-pass rate at $368** versus **14% at $954** for pure Opus — nearly the same or better quality at 2.6× lower cost. SFT-tuned Kimi 2.6 went from 11% to 15% all-pass at ~11× lower cost than Opus.

**Why it matters to her audience:** The "just use the best model" era is ending for anyone serious. These aren't hypothetical benchmarks — they're from production legal AI workloads. The insight: routing (cheap worker + smart advisor) isn't just a cost trick, it's better. And org-level spend caps make cost-awareness a job skill, not just an IT problem.

## Quick Scan

- **Project Glasswing → 150 orgs, 15+ countries:** Anthropic's AI security program (using Claude Mythos) expanded from ~50 to 150 partner organizations in power, water, healthcare, and comms sectors. Initial cohort found **10,000+ high/critical severity security flaws** — this is real AI-assisted vulnerability discovery at scale.

- **"The framework era is ending" — Jerry Liu (LlamaIndex):** Abstractions are moving upward from Python wrappers into skills, context quality, and execution layers. A signal that the current Python-orchestration tooling (LangChain etc.) is being superseded — worth watching for where the next layer forms.

- **Microsoft MAI-Thinking-1:** Microsoft's first in-house reasoning model (35B active params, sparse MoE, 256K context) trained entirely on commercially licensed data — **no distillation from OpenAI or other third-party models**. Hits 97% AIME 2025, preferred over Claude Sonnet 4.6 in blind evals. Available in private preview via Microsoft Foundry.

- **Ideogram 4.0 goes open weights:** Layout-aware image generation (previously a hard problem) is now open and commercially usable — ranked #1 among open image models on Arena. Strong for text rendering and branding design.

- **Gemma 4 12B (Apache 2.0):** Google's multimodal model runs on 8GB RAM quantized, no separate vision tower — raw audio and images project directly into text-token space. Immediate support in Ollama, llama.cpp, MLX. Local AI becoming a real deployment option.

- **Windsurf rebranded to Devin Desktop (June 2):** A brand signal — Windsurf is leaning into autonomous/agentic framing rather than "coding assistant."

- **Cursor Teams pricing restructured:** Standard seats $32/seat/mo (annual) or $40/mo; new Premium seats $96/seat/mo with 5× usage. Claude Code continues displacing Cursor among enterprise developers per multiple reports.

- **Claude for Legal officially launched:** 90+ legal AI agents live — Vendor Agreement Reviewer, DSAR Responder, Claim Chart Builder, etc. Vertical AI with full workflows is here.

- **Axiom Math (Verified AI):** Axiom ($1.6B valuation, $200M Series A) hit **99% on Verina** (formal code correctness benchmark) vs OpenAI o3 at 4.9%.
