Third-party power users and indie toolmakers are now shopping. Your audience — the ones running agentic workflows — needs to understand this isn't just a pricing story, it's a platform strategy shift. ### 3. A supply chain attack is hiding in Claude Code and VS Code settings files
**What it is:** The Mini Shai-Hulud supply chain campaign expanded from TanStack to hit Mistral AI, Guardrails AI, UiPath, OpenSearch packages across npm and PyPI. The technically alarming detail: it hooks into `.claude/settings.json` and `.vscode/tasks.json`, so the compromise can re-execute on future tool events *even after you remove the malicious package.* Guardrails AI confirmed its 0.10.1 package was compromised; quarantined within 2 hours.

**Why it matters:** This is the first high-profile attack that specifically targets the AI developer workflow layer. Anyone who installed an affected package and uses Claude Code or VS Code should audit their settings files. The audience should know: the attack surface has expanded from "what packages you install" to "what your AI tools do on every run." Actionable mitigation: enable `blockExoticSubdeps`, move secrets out of `.env` into a proper secrets manager, audit `.claude/settings.json`.

**Format:** Tip (with urgency)

**Reel angle:** "There's a supply chain attack hiding in your Claude Code settings — here's what to check right now"
