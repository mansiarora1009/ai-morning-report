### 1. Claude Opus 5.5 is live — and the writing actually changed

It performs at Fable 5.1 level for most tasks, costs 40% less and runs ~30% faster than Opus 5 (at default/medium effort). The headline technical change is writing behavior: it front-loads the most important information and obeys user writing rules. Anthropic staff posted "way, way, way better than Opus 5. Sorry about that model." Em dashes are gone.

**Why it matters to your audience:** This is the model people are actually using in Claude Code and the Claude app starting today — it's the new default everywhere. The practical change isn't benchmarks; it's that old prompt tricks now work against you. Anthropic published a new prompting playbook: hand over a whole task and define "done," drop "think carefully" (model always thinks first), and after a long run ask what it needs to go further.

### 2. Effort levels are not what you think — medium beats xhigh on coding
**What it is:** Third-party evals revealed a counterintuitive finding: on agentic coding tasks, xhigh effort costs ~2.8x more than medium effort for a *lower* score. The explanation: higher effort produces scope creep, and benchmarks like FrontierCode penalize unnecessary changes. Practitioners are advising: stick with medium.

**Why it matters:** Most power users assume "more effort = better." This flips that assumption for a huge use case (coding). It's also directly actionable — change one setting, spend less money, get better results.

**Format:** Tip
**Reel angle:** "The expensive Claude setting that actually makes it worse at coding"
