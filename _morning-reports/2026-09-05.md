### 1. GPT-6 Astra: OpenAI's biggest model launch — and what the fine print reveals
**What it is:** OpenAI launched GPT-6 Astra (Sept 3-4), its new flagship targeting computer use, long-horizon software engineering, and math/science. Claims 99.9% on ARC-AGI-3 (via special harness), 98% on FrontierMath Tier 4, and a 30-minute autonomous task horizon vs. 3.6 min for its predecessor. Pricing: $10/$50 per million tokens (standard), $20/$100 (fast).

**Why it matters to the audience:** The benchmarks are real but highly harness-sensitive. Astra is 70% more token-efficient than GPT-5.6 Sol but ~2.5x more expensive per task at max effort. Independent evals (Artificial Analysis) put it level with Claude Opus 5 and Fable 5 on coding — not clearly ahead. **The hidden story:** OpenAI's system card acknowledges Astra has *decreased chain-of-thought monitorability* — its reasoning summaries can be missing up to 80% of its actual reasoning on long tasks. More capable, but harder to audit.
