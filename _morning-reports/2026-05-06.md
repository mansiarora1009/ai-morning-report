### 3. The Harness Is the Product Now (Not the Model)
**What it is:** A recurring theme in developer communities this week: model quality alone no longer explains agent performance. The real differentiator is **model × harness × task fit** — the instructions, tools, context packing, and feedback loops wrapped around the model. Cursor now ships agents that automatically monitor GitHub and fix CI failures. Codex is winning on UX. Claude Code is losing ground in some comparisons despite strong underlying models.

**Why it matters to your audience:** If you're building with AI, you're probably over-indexing on which model to use. The actual leverage is in how you structure your context, what tools you give the model, and how you measure and close the loop. This is the practical skill that separates people who get consistent results from people who vibe-code their way to frustration.

**Format:** Explainer

**Reel angle:** "The model is almost irrelevant — here's what actually makes AI agents work."
