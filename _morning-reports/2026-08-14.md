### 2. Claude Is Now Watermarking Everything It Writes — Here's How It Actually Works
**What it is:** Anthropic rolled out invisible text watermarks in Claude outputs starting August 2, 2026. New Claude models embed a model-level watermark designed to survive copy-paste and light editing without changing readability. Files (PNG, JPG, SVG) get digitally signed C2PA provenance metadata. The watermark works via keyed token-sampling bias: during generation, the model slightly boosts a secret, context-dependent subset of tokens, creating a detectable statistical pattern. Detection checks whether "favored" tokens appear above chance using a z-score-style statistic.

**Why it matters:** This is the first production-scale AI output watermarking rollout. It reframes the AI-generated content detection conversation — the debate is no longer theoretical. The technical mechanism (sampling bias, not hidden characters) is fascinating and counterintuitive. The caveat: paraphrasing through another model likely destroys the signal, which itself becomes the story.
