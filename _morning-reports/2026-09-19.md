## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. Jev — Discriminative Decision Models Are Having Their Moment
**What it is:** Jev is a non-generative decision model — think "System 1" for AI stacks. Instead of generating text, it scores options and outputs calibrated probabilities (which path to escalate, which tool to call, which route to take). It launched with 36M views on X in two days, and 6 open-source clones appeared within 48 hours (Bespoke Nimble, Kev-0.5B, DiffusionGemmaJev, SemIf, Laya, Jevlike).

**Why it matters to your audience:** This isn't a chatbot upgrade — it's a new architectural layer in how people build AI workflows. The people doing the most sophisticated agent work are routing decisions *away* from expensive generative LLMs toward fast, on-device discriminative models. Use cases: routing, citation selection, escalation, browser-use control, notification filtering. Kev-0.5B runs on a MacBook Pro. The "aha" is that you don't need a full LLM to make a decision.

**Format:** Shift

**Reel angle:** "Everyone's talking about which LLM to use. The actual move people are making right now: a tiny decision model sitting in front of your LLM doing routing for near-zero cost. Here's what Jev is and why 6 clones appeared in 48 hours."

### 3. Harness Design Beats Model Choice for Coding Agents
**What it is:** A new paper (An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents) shows benchmark outcomes are shaped more by harness structure — tool set, context setup, turn budgets — than by the base model. Key finding: a minimal 4-tool set (read, write, edit, bash) hits the Pareto frontier on performance while cutting unnecessary spend. Separately, practitioners reported routing planning to frontier models and execution to cheap ones, cutting pipeline costs by ~100x (Opus → Sonnet → GLM 5.3 Flash).

**Why it matters to your audience:** Most people optimizing AI coding are asking "which model?" The people getting real leverage are asking "how is the harness designed?" This is the difference between using an AI tool and *building* one. The cost bifurcation data (100x reduction) is immediately actionable.

**Format:** Explainer

**Reel angle:** "You're probably picking the wrong variable when you upgrade your AI coding setup. It's not the model — it's the harness. Here's what that means and what the research actually shows."
