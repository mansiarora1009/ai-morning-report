### 2. The inner loop vs. outer loop debate — what it means for your work
**What it is:** At AI Engineer World's Fair (Day 3, July 1), two competing frameworks emerged. Roland Gavrilescu (Introspection) introduced "autoresearch" — agents running a loop that studies and maintains the system itself (the outer loop goes to the agent). Addy Osmani (ex-Google) pushed back hard: "Agents can run much more of the inner execution loop. But that outer loop is still engineering." Geoffrey Litt (Notion) went furthest: *"Those who delegate understanding will be replaced by the agent."*

**Why it matters:** This is the cleanest framing of the real stakes — not whether AI can code, but which loop you're in. It's also concrete enough to explain: inner loop = do the tasks; outer loop = decide what to do and why.

**Format:** Explainer

**Reel angle:** *"There are two loops in AI-assisted work. The one you give up might be the one that keeps you employed."*
