### 2. Adding a skill library to your AI agent can quietly make it worse
**What it is:** A new paper introduced Retrieval-Invoked Actual-Use Effect (RIAUE) — a matched evaluation method that runs the same task twice: once with skill retrieval enabled, once without. It only counts results where retrieval actually fired. Across 17 LLMs on coding and math, skill retrieval improved aggregate benchmark scores while having a negative same-task effect on the exact tasks where it was used.

**Why it matters:** This is a practical trap that's easy to fall into right now. If you're evaluating your agent's tool library by looking at average performance, you can get a false positive — retrieval looks useful in aggregate while actively hurting the tasks it runs on. For anyone building agents with RAG, skill directories, or tool libraries, the takeaway is: measure performance specifically on the tasks that trigger retrieval, not overall averages.

**Format:** Explainer

**Reel angle:** "Adding tools to your AI agent might be making it worse — here's the counterintuitive research finding and what to do instead"
