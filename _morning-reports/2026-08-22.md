### 2. The Agent Harness Is Becoming a Human Attention Interface
**What it is:** Latent Space published a detailed essay tracing the evolution of agent harnesses: from ReAct prompting (2022) → AutoGPT's premature autonomy → Cursor's human-in-the-loop retreat → Claude Code's "curves cross" moment (Feb 2025) → today's co-training era. The key finding: HarnessBench showed that swapping harnesses on the *same model* produced a 23.8-point spread on agent tasks (52.4 → 76.2). Models are now absorbing harness capabilities into weights (Claude Code deleted 80% of its system prompt). What's left will be an "attention interface" — governing *when* agents interrupt you, not *what* they can do.

**Why it matters:** Half the capability of an AI agent is the harness, not the model. And the harness is about to flip from being scaffolding for the model to being scaffolding for *your attention*. This reframes how people should think about building with AI.

**Format:** Explainer — makes the vague concept of "agent harness" finally make sense; Shift — points to where agent design is going next.

**Reel angle:** "The same model scored 52 in one agent harness and 76 in another. Here's why the wrapper matters as much as the model — and what that means for how you build."

### 3. Simile AI: Digital Twins Are Replacing Focus Groups ($2B Series B)
**What it is:** Simile AI (founded by Joon Sung Park, creator of the landmark Generative Agents / Smallville paper) raised a $2B Series B backed by Fei-Fei Li and Andrej Karpathy. They're running tens of millions of AI human simulations for Fortune 100 clients (e.g. CVS), achieving 85–99% accuracy vs actual human focus groups. The technical insight: standard frontier models are bad at simulating real people because they're trained to be *agents*, not humans — so Simile post-trains on biographical interviews and registered RCTs to recover human bias, inconsistency, and causal texture.

**Why it matters:** Focus groups, A/B tests, and user studies are becoming inference workloads. This is a concrete, immediately graspable example of AI replacing a human process — and it's already live at enterprise scale.

**Format:** Shift — a bigger pattern changing how companies work with AI.

**Reel angle:** "A company just replaced human focus groups with AI — 85-99% accuracy, millions of simulations. Here's how digital twins actually work."
