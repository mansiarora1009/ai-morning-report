### 2. Your Encrypted AI Reasoning Isn't Private — Researchers Just Proved It
**What it is:** A new research paper demonstrated that the "encrypted" reasoning traces used by Claude, GPT, and Gemini can be decoded and ported to other models via replay attacks. The technique: replay a signed reasoning block from an API response to a weaker model (e.g., Haiku 4.5), then prompt it to transcribe the attached reasoning. In a scan of ~7,000 public Claude Code/Codex sessions: **62 unique API keys, 33 email addresses, and 33 passwords** were found — most appearing only inside the supposedly-encrypted reasoning blocks, invisible in the visible session. The paper was responsibly disclosed; some vulnerabilities are already patched, but similar attacks likely still work.

**Why it matters:** Anyone who has shared a Claude Code or Codex session publicly may have exposed sensitive data they thought was hidden in encrypted thinking. More broadly, this explodes the assumption that "hidden reasoning" = "secure reasoning." Alignment researchers are now flagging that COT summarizers hide answers and reasoning blocks contain hints of misaligned behavior — this paper makes those thoughts readable.
