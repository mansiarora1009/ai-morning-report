## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. GPT-5.6 reframes what "using more compute" means — and it's not what you think
**What it is:** OpenAI launched GPT-5.6 as a three-tier family (Luna / Terra / Sol). "Max effort" doesn't mean a bigger model — it means the *same model running longer*, or in Sol's case, *automatically spawning parallel subagents* to plan, verify, and act simultaneously.

**Why it matters:** Most people still think of AI as a Q&A machine where you pick a smarter model for harder tasks. GPT-5.6 forces a conceptual upgrade: the *harness* is now the product. Sol doesn't get smarter — it gets more parallel. That's a fundamentally different mental model for what "powerful AI" means.

**Format:** Explainer

**Reel angle:** "Why GPT-5.6 Sol feels smarter — it's not what you think"

### 2. Anthropic shipped "Reflect with Claude" — a way to see how you actually use Claude
**What it is:** A new feature that shows users patterns in how they've been using Claude — surfacing the gap between how people think they use AI vs. how they actually do.

**Why it matters:** This is the first mainstream move toward AI self-awareness tooling for *users*, not models. If Claude can show you that you keep asking it the same kind of questions in inefficient ways, that's a new category of productivity insight.

**Format:** First Step

**Reel angle:** "Claude now shows you your own AI usage patterns — here's how to check"

### 3. Multi-agent workflows just became a standard API primitive
**What it is:** GPT-5.6 adds "Multi-agent" as a first-class API feature — models can spin up focused subagents directly without any custom orchestration layer. Meanwhile, Claude Code's Managed Agents architecture ("decoupling the brain from the hands") is shipping the same pattern from the other direction.

**Why it matters:** Six months ago, multi-agent systems were a custom engineering project. Now they're a checkbox in the API call. Anyone building AI workflows who hasn't thought about this architecture is already behind the default.

**Format:** Shift

**Reel angle:** "Multi-agent just became the default — not the advanced setting"
