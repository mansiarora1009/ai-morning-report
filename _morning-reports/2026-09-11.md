## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. GPT-6 Astra is an agent, not a chat model — the API framing tells you everything
**What it is:** OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra (launched Sep 3) isn't positioned as a smarter chatbot — it's an autonomous computer-use agent. It opens browsers, edits spreadsheets, reviews codebases across dozens of files, and runs multi-step jobs over days without a human supervising each step. Available via `client.responses.create` (not `chat.completions`), $10/$50 per million input/output tokens. Also available on Azure and Bedrock.

**Why it matters:** The API surface is the tell. OpenAI has migrated away from the chat paradigm at the model layer. For people in tech who still think of AI as "write this for me," this is the line between the old mental model and the new one. The question is no longer "what prompt do I write?" — it's "what task do I hand off entirely?"

### 2. Claudeforce is live in beta — Claude now has your CRM
**What it is:** Salesforce and Anthropic's Claudeforce partnership launched Salesforce in Claude this month: a plugin with 37 prebuilt sales skills that connect Claude directly to live Salesforce data. Sellers can reason over pipeline context, automate CRM updates, and take governed actions — all from inside Claude. Open beta hitting in September. Separate from this, Claude powers Slackbot for all Salesforce customers: 8.1M hours of annualized productivity gains, 2x quarter-over-quarter.

**Why it matters:** This is what "AI-native CRM" looks like in practice. Not "ask AI to draft a follow-up email" — but "Claude sees your deal history, your call notes, your open tasks and can update the CRM and draft the email in one move." For sales and ops people in tech, this is the most concrete example yet of AI collapsing a multi-tool workflow into a single conversation.

### 3. Anthropic introduced two-tier model naming: Fable vs Mythos
**What it is:** On September 1, Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1 — described as identical models with different safeguard levels. Fable is for general coding, knowledge work, and scientific research. Mythos is a higher-capability tier. Same underlying model, different operational guardrails. Claude Sonnet 5 promotional pricing also ended Sep 1 ($2→$3 / $10→$15 per million tokens).

**Why it matters:** The Fable/Mythos split is the first time Anthropic has explicitly named a two-tier safety model where the difference isn't capability but constraint. For practitioners choosing models, this reframes the question from "which model is smartest" to "which safeguard level does my use case require." This is how enterprise AI governance actually works at scale.
