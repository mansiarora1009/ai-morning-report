## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. Jev and the Decision Model wave — the "System 1" for AI workflows
**What it is:** Jev is a non-generative decision model that went viral this week (36M views in 2 days). It's not a chatbot — it's a discriminative model trained to classify options, route decisions, and score choices at near-zero cost. Within 48 hours, 6 open-source clones appeared (Bespoke Nimble, SemIf, Kev-0.5B, Jevlike, DiffusionGemmaJev, Laya).

**Why it matters to your audience:** This is a new systems primitive. The practical wins are real: ~400x cheaper scoring than LLMs for evals, on-device routing, and browser-use workflows that combine Jev for decisions + an LLM for generation. The pattern emerging: LLMs as slow, expensive System 2 reasoning; decision models as fast, cheap System 1 judgment.

### 3. Harness design matters more than which model you pick
**What it is:** New research ("Harness Tax" analysis + *An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents*) shows that benchmark outcomes for coding agents are shaped more by harness structure — tool set, context setup, turn budgets — than by the underlying model. A simple 4-tool setup (read/write/edit/bash) reaches the Pareto frontier. Separately, practitioners are now routinely running 2-model stacks: frontier model for planning, cheap model for execution, with teams reporting ~100x cost reduction.

**Why it matters to your audience:** Most people are chasing better models. The real unlock is already in their hands — it's how they wire up the tools, context, and routing. This reframes AI productivity as a systems design problem, not a model-picking problem.

**Format:** Shift
**Reel angle:** *"Stop optimizing for the best AI model. The people getting 100x more out of AI figured out something different."*
