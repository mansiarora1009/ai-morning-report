## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. ChatGPT Work is a coding agent wearing a knowledge-work suit
**What it is:** An external reconstruction (swyx, Aug 4) reveals that ChatGPT Work — which crossed 10M users in its first 3 weeks — runs on the Codex agent harness. Each session runs in an isolated microVM with real compute (Pro: 8 CPUs, 20 GB RAM, 64 GB disk), a managed Chrome instance for genuine browser use, and plugin connections to Slack, email, Drive, calendars, and hundreds of other tools. Outputs aren't text — they're interactive Sheets, Docs, Slides, and hosted "Sites" that it builds and keeps updated autonomously.

**Why it matters:** OpenAI has shipped a full agentic compute environment to knowledge workers without calling it an agent. Greg Brockman confirmed Work and regular ChatGPT will merge by year-end — meaning this infrastructure becomes the default for ~1 billion weekly users. The reframe: people who think they're upgrading their chat tool are actually adopting an autonomous work system.

The implied read: scale and bureaucracy are incompatible with the kind of high-agency, small-team research these people want to do. The structural question of *where* the best AI work happens is getting answered in real time — and it's not inside big companies.

### 3. New model economics: the practical hierarchy is finally clear
**What it is:** Opus 5 launched at roughly half the price of Claude's previous flagship. The emerging practitioner rule: default to Sonnet 5, escalate to Opus 5 when the task is genuinely hard (complex agentic coding, long-doc analysis, multi-step reasoning), reach for Fable 5 only when you've measured that Opus 5 isn't enough. In coding tooling, the split is: Cursor 3 for daily in-editor flow, Claude Code on Opus 5 for big refactors and autonomous multi-step tasks.

**Why it matters:** For the first time there's a legible hierarchy that costs-in naturally. Most people are either defaulting to the most expensive model (wasting money) or staying on weaker models out of habit (leaving capability on the table). Knowing the routing rule is a genuine workflow upgrade.
