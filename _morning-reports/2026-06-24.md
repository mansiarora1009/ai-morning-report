## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. Claude Tag — AI moves into Slack as a persistent, proactive teammate
**What it is:** Anthropic launched Claude Tag, a Slack-native agent that is multiplayer (a whole team can tag it), persistent (it remembers context across conversations), and proactive (it surfaces things without being asked). The AINews newsletter calls it the "third major redesign of LLM UIUX" — Web → Desktop → Slack.

**Why it matters:** This isn't a chatbot plugin. Claude Tag can monitor an A/B test, track a metric with guardrails, alert the team mid-run, and show up at the end with a rollout PR already built. The workflow loop closes *inside Slack*. Anthropic also says Claude Code's Tag form now merges 65% of their internal product PRs.

**Format:** Shift — this is the moment AI moves from a tool you visit to a teammate that shows up.

**Reel angle:** "The AI that shows up in Slack with the PR already done — this is what async agents actually look like in practice."

### 2. Cursor adds a team marketplace for MCPs/plugins/skills
**What it is:** Cursor launched a team-level marketplace where organizations can share plugins, skills, and MCP servers. Your team curates its own tool layer that every developer in the org gets access to.

**Why it matters:** Until now, MCP was an individual power-user thing. Making it organizational means companies will start standardizing *how their AI-powered developers work*. The harness layer — what tools your AI can reach — is becoming an org-level decision, not a personal one.

**Format:** Shift — the move from individual AI setups to company-wide AI stacks.

**Reel angle:** "Your company's AI stack is about to look like your company's dev tools stack — curated, shared, and enforced."
