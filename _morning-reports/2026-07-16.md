## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. Thinking Machines Lab drops Inkling — biggest American open-weight model, Apache 2.0
**What it is:** Mira Murati's post-OpenAI team (also includes John Schulman, Soumith Chintala, Lilian Weng) quietly released "Inkling" — a 975B total / 41B active MoE model with multimodal input (text, image, audio), 1M token context, trained from scratch. Free on Hugging Face; hosted on their Tinker platform at $1.87/1M input tokens. Day-0 support from vLLM, SGLang, Modal, Baseten, Databricks, and Hugging Face. A smaller Inkling-Small (12B active) also previewed.

**Why it matters to the audience:** This is the most credible American open-weight release ever — from the team that built much of OpenAI. Apache 2.0 means fine-tunable, self-hostable, and enterprise-ready without licensing drama. If you want a customizable LLM without paying Anthropic or OpenAI royalties, this is the new default conversation.

**Format:** Shift  
**Reel angle:** "The most important open AI model drop of 2026 just happened — by the woman who was almost OpenAI's CEO."

### 2. AI engineers have a new job title: harness engineer
**What it is:** Swyx's recap of the AI Engineering World's Fair 2026 crystallized 5 trends: (1) harness engineering — building the system around the agent — replaces prompt engineering as the core skill; (2) "loop engineering" becomes the dominant mental model (outer loop = human sets direction, runs evals; inner loop = agent executes autonomously); (3) Skills — declarative markdown files encoding senior-engineer workflows — are the new abstraction replacing Python orchestration code; (4) coding agents have replaced the IDE as the primary dev interface; (5) enterprise adoption is FDE-driven, with "software factories" running long-horizon pipelines.

**Why it matters to the audience:** This is the clearest map of what the job actually looks like now. Not "prompt better." Build a feedback system that keeps agents on track. Your value is the outer loop.

**Format:** Explainer  
**Reel angle:** "Your job isn't writing code anymore. Here's what it actually is."

### 3. Stale agents.md = self-inflicted prompt injection
**What it is:** In the July 14 AINews issue (buried under a "not much happened" headline), swyx flagged a critical practical insight: outdated `agents.md` / `CLAUDE.md` instruction files act like self-inflicted prompt injection — they confuse long-running coding agents into stalls that can waste hours. Separately, Codex hit 6M active users with 1M new users joining in a single day (vs. Claude Code's 2M reported in February).

**Why it matters to the audience:** Completely actionable. If you're using Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor on a repo with stale instruction files, you're actively sabotaging your agent. The fix is 10 minutes of maintenance that most people aren't doing.

**Format:** Tip  
**Reel angle:** "Your coding agent is broken and you probably caused it. Here's the 10-minute fix."
