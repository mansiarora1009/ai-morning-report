### 3. Prompting Claude 4.7 (Opus 4.7) is fundamentally different from 4.6 — and most people are doing it wrong
**What it is:** Anthropic published a 31-page prompting guide for Opus 4.7. Newsletter writer Ruben (500k subscribers) condensed it to 7 concrete behavioral changes: 4.7 is more literal (scope every output explicitly), sizes answers to task complexity (cap length or it'll go long), ignores negative instructions (use positives only), calls fewer tools by default (reason more between calls), dropped the warm/validating tone, requires action verbs over vague asks, and responds to "go beyond the basics" on creative work.

**Why it matters:** People using Opus 4.7 with 4.6 prompting habits are getting worse results than they should. The model genuinely behaves differently — less hand-holding, more literal execution. The mental model shift: stop describing what you don't want, start specifying exactly what output you need. Trick to surface max reasoning: add "Think before answering (maximum reasoning)" at the end of any prompt.

**Format:** Tip

**Reel angle:** "Your Claude prompts from last month are broken — here are the 7 things that changed in Opus 4.7"
