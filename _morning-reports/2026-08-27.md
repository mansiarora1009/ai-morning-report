## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. GLM-5.3-Flash: The open model that just ate 11% of Cline's traffic in a week
**What it is:** Z.ai launched GLM-5.3-Flash (previously teased as "Ox Alpha") — 320B total/18B active params, 1M-token context, MIT-licensed, natively multimodal, running entirely on Chinese chips. Third-party benchmark: $0.09/task vs $0.68 for the full GLM-5.3. Cline says it's the fastest-growing model in their history, hitting 11% of all traffic within days.

**Why it matters:** This isn't a benchmarks story — it's a usage story. Engineers moved to it *fast*, for real work. The "best intelligence per dollar" framing is landing. And it runs on Chinese chips, which is the quietly bigger deal.

### 2. Lovable's bet: every app gets two interfaces — one for humans, one for AI
**What it is:** Lovable (now $13.3B valuation, $500M ARR) is pivoting toward "capabilities" — taking any app built on their platform and exposing it as an MCP server so agents (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) can call it directly without a human UI. Their framing: a "company brain" that routes work to the right capability, not the right app.

**Why it matters:** This is the clearest articulation yet of where SaaS is going. Not "AI-powered apps" — apps that *are* tools for AI. The CTO's quote nails it: "SaaS businesses are going to have to focus more on providing the shovel for AI to use their capabilities." That's a mental model shift.

**Format:** Explainer / Shift
**Reel angle:** "Lovable just explained why every SaaS app needs to become an MCP server. Here's what that actually means."

### 3. NVIDIA buys HuggingFace for $13B
**What it is:** NVIDIA confirmed the acquisition of HuggingFace at $13B (~80x $150M ARR), nearly double their January 2026 offer. HuggingFace doubled its customer base in 2026.

**Why it matters:** The hardware layer just bought the open-model distribution layer. NVIDIA now owns the place where most people download weights, run models, and share datasets. That's a massive concentration of power — and raises questions about what "open" means when NVIDIA holds the hub.

**Format:** Shift
**Reel angle:** "NVIDIA just bought the place where everyone gets their AI models. Here's why that matters more than the price tag."
