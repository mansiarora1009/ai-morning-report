### 1. Thinking Machines dropped a model that replaces the turn-based paradigm entirely
**What it is:** Mira Murati's Thinking Machines released TML-Interaction-Small (276B MoE, 12B active), a model trained from scratch for continuous real-time interaction — not layered voice on top of a text LLM. It processes audio+video+text simultaneously in 200ms chunks, handles interruptions natively, speaks proactively ("count my push-ups", "tell me when I start slouching"), and runs background tool calls without announcing it's doing so.

**Why it matters to Mansi's audience:** Every current "realtime" voice product — including GPT-4o voice and Gemini Live — is fundamentally a text LLM with a speech wrapper bolted on. This is the first architecture that treats continuous human↔AI interaction as the primary design constraint. The demos aren't polished consumer products, but they're a clear signal of where the interface ceiling is moving. People who understand the distinction between "voice-wrapped LLM" and "interaction model" will have a big head start explaining what the next wave of AI products actually is.
