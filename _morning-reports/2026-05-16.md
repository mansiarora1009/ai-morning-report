### 2. Screen-record your feedback to agents (immediately try this)
**What it is:** Ben's Bites shared a workflow: instead of typing feedback to a coding agent, screen-record yourself talking through what you want while navigating to examples. Drop the video file to the agent. It extracts keyframes, transcribes your voice, generates GIFs for static UI points, and builds an HTML report with an action checklist. Ben packaged it as a one-paragraph Claude skill (`video-to-html`).

**Why it matters:** Most people give agents vague text prompts. This closes the gap between "I know what I want" and "the agent understands what I want" — especially for UI/design feedback where showing is faster than describing. It's an immediately repeatable workflow, not a research finding.

**Format:** Tip

**Reel angle:** "Stop typing feedback to your AI agent — screen record it instead (here's the exact skill)"

### 3. Anthropic is splitting Claude's rate limits — here's what changes June 15
**What it is:** Claude plans are getting a new split: using Claude through first-party surfaces (Claude.ai, Claude Code app) stays the same. Using Claude through third-party tools — Conductor, Zed, T3 Code, Openclaw, etc. — gets a separate monthly credit equal to your plan value, then billed at API rates with no rollover. Anthropic is softening the landing by raising weekly limits 50% for two months. Effective June 15.

**Why it matters:** Power users who've been running Claude-backed apps through third-party wrappers at flat subscription rates are about to hit a wall. The practical implication: your routing decisions now have direct cost consequences. This is the moment to understand which surface you're actually using Claude through — and why it matters.
