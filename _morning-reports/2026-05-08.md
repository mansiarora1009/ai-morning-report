## Top 3 Things Worth Paying Attention To

### 1. GPT-Realtime-2: Voice agents crossed the "real work" threshold
**What it is:** OpenAI launched GPT-Realtime-2, GPT-Realtime-Translate, and GPT-Realtime-Whisper in the Realtime API. The flagship brings GPT-5-class reasoning to real-time voice: tool calls mid-conversation, 128K context (4x increase from 32K), adjustable reasoning effort (minimal → xhigh), graceful interruption recovery, and audible tool-call transparency ("checking your calendar"). Translate supports live speech-to-speech from 70+ input languages into 13 output languages. Pricing unchanged at $1.15/hr input, $4.61/hr output.

**Why it matters:** Practitioner consensus from Genspark (+26% conversation completion rate), Glean (+42.9% helpfulness), and voice agent builders is the same: this is the first S2S model good enough for agents doing real work. The usability features matter more than the benchmark jump — preambles, state recovery, longer sessions, and visible tool-call narration make the UX actually functional. And the design implication is significant: voice apps are now stateful real-time systems with latency budgets and failure recovery, not chatbots with a microphone bolted on. Note: ChatGPT voice mode itself has not received the upgrade yet (consumer rollout pending).
